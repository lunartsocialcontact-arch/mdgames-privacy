MD Games Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile games published by MD Games ("we", "us") collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to every game listed in section 11 ("our games"). Contact: lunart.social.contact@gmail.com.

1. No account, no personal details

Our games do not ask for your name, e-mail address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location. You play without creating an account. Where a game saves progress, leaderboard scores or settings online, an anonymous player ID is created for your installation; leaderboards show an automatically generated nickname, never your real name.

2. Information collected automatically

InformationUsed for
Device and advertising identifiers (for example Android Advertising ID, Apple IDFA where you allow tracking, app instance ID)Analytics, showing and measuring ads
Gameplay events (levels played, progress, items used, screens visited, buttons tapped)Understanding and improving our games
Purchase history (which in-game product was bought and its price; never your card details)Delivering purchases, analytics
Device information (model, operating system, language, country derived from IP address)Analytics, ads, fixing problems
Error and performance reportsFixing crashes and bugs

Payments are processed entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never receive your payment details.

3. Third-party services

Depending on the game, we use some of the following services, which process data under their own privacy policies:

  • Google Firebase / Google Analytics for Firebase — analytics — https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • GameAnalytics — analytics — https://gameanalytics.com/privacy
  • Unity Gaming Services (Authentication, Leaderboards, Remote Config) and Unity IAP — game services and purchases — https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • Unity LevelPlay and the ad networks it uses (for example Unity Ads) — advertising — https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
  • Google Play Billing and Apple App Store — payments

4. Advertising

Our games may show ads, including optional rewarded ads you choose to watch. Ad partners may use your advertising identifier to show ads that are more relevant to you and to measure them.

  • Opting out on Android: Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID (or opt out of ads personalization).
  • Opting out on iOS: our games ask permission before tracking; you can change it any time in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • EU/UK users: where required, we ask for your consent before personalized advertising, and you can change your choice later in the game.
  • "No Ads" purchases remove the ads described in that game's store; rewarded ads stay optional.

5. Children

Our games are not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will delete it.

6. Retention and deletion

Analytics data is kept for up to 14 months. Because our games have no accounts, data is linked only to device and anonymous identifiers. To request deletion of data associated with your device, e-mail lunart.social.contact@gmail.com with the name of the game, and we will process the request within 30 days. Step-by-step instructions: Data Deletion Request. Uninstalling a game removes the data it stored on your device.

7. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS).

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/UK under GDPR, California under CCPA/CPRA, Türkiye under KVKK), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data, and to withdraw consent. Contact us at lunart.social.contact@gmail.com.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. The "Last updated" date shows the latest version; significant changes will be announced in the games or on their store pages.
