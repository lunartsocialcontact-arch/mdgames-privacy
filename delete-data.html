Data Deletion Request

Cats Need Naps · developer: MD Games · last updated: November 4, 2026

This page explains how to ask MD Games to delete the data collected by Cats Need Naps. The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, e-mail or phone number. The data it collects is linked only to anonymous device and player identifiers (see our Privacy Policy).

How to request deletion

  1. Send an e-mail to lunart.social.contact@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion - Cats Need Naps".
  2. In the e-mail, tell us your device model and roughly when you played. This helps us find the data linked to your device. We may reply to ask for details that help us identify it. We will never ask for a password or payment details.
  3. We confirm the request and complete the deletion within 30 days, then reply to tell you it is done.

You can also delete some data yourself, right away:

  • Game progress on your device (levels, stars, coins, settings) is deleted when you uninstall the game.
  • Advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings → Google → Ads → Delete advertising ID. This disconnects ad networks from your past activity.

What may be kept, and for how long

  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play, not by us, under Google's own policies. We may keep transaction records for as long as tax and accounting law requires.
  • Aggregated, anonymous statistics (for example "how many players finished level 10") can't be linked back to you, so they are kept.
  • Advertising networks (LevelPlay, Unity Ads, AppLovin, Google AdMob) hold their own data under their own privacy policies. Deleting or resetting your advertising ID cuts the link to it.
  • Analytics data that isn't deleted on request is removed automatically after 14 months at the latest.
